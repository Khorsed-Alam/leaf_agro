## 2. Three Types of Treatment Options

### A. Cultural Management (মাঠ ব্যবস্থাপনা)
Non-chemical, agronomic practices that halt disease spread:
- Pruning and burning infected plant debris.
- Adjusting irrigation to prevent stagnant water.
- Spacing plants properly to improve sunlight penetration and air circulation.

### B. Organic & Biological Solutions (জৈব সমাধান)
Eco-friendly remedies safe for beneficial insects:
- Spraying diluted Neem seed oil extract (5ml per liter of water).
- Applying bio-fungicides like *Trichoderma viride*.
- Using wood ash around plant stems to deter soil-borne fungi.

### C. Chemical Interventions (রাসায়নিক চিকিৎসা)
Registered fungicides and bactericides for severe outbreaks:
- Exact active ingredients (e.g., Mancozeb 75% WP, Copper Oxychloride 50% WP).
- Precise mixing ratios (e.g., 2 grams per 1 liter of clean water).
- Spraying technique: spray thoroughly on both upper and lower leaf surfaces during cool hours.

---

## 3. Safe Agrochemical Handling
1. **Wear Protective Gear**: Always wear a face mask, eye goggles, and rubber gloves when handling and spraying chemicals.
2. **Pre-Harvest Intervals (PHI)**: Do not harvest crops for consumption within 7 to 14 days of spraying chemical pesticides.
3. **Proper Disposal**: Wash spray tanks away from ponds, canals, and livestock drinking water sources.
