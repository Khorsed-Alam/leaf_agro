# AgriCare AI — Profile and Settings Guide

## 1. User Profile Screen (Screen 24)
Tap the **"Profile"** icon in the bottom navigation bar to open your farmer profile:
- **Profile Picture**: Tap the camera icon on your avatar to upload a new profile picture.
- **Farmer Details**: Displays your full name, email, phone number, and registered district.
- **Detection Stats**: Shows the total number of scans you have performed and your active crops.
- **Quick Links**: Fast shortcuts to Settings, Language Selection, Help & FAQ, and Feedback.

---

## 2. Settings Screen (Screen 25)
In the Settings screen, you can manage your preferences:
- **Language**: Switch between English and Bengali (বাংলা).
- **Notifications**: Enable or disable scan reminders and agronomic advisory notifications.
- **Appearance**: Toggle between Light Mode and Dark Mode.
- **Offline AI Model**: View technical specifications of your on-device MobileNetV2 model.
- **Storage & Cache**: View cached image storage and clear local temporary files to free up phone storage.
- **Privacy Policy & Terms**: Read the verified terms of service and data privacy commitments.
- **Log Out**: Safely sign out of your account with a confirmation prompt.
