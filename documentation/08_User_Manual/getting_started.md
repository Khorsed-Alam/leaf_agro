# AgriCare AI — Getting Started

## 1. System Requirements
To run AgriCare AI smoothly on your mobile device:
- **Operating System**: Android 7.0 (Nougat) or higher (Android 8.0+ recommended).
- **RAM**: Minimum 2 GB of RAM (3 GB+ recommended).
- **Storage**: Minimum 100 MB of available device storage.
- **Camera**: 8 MP or higher rear camera with autofocus.
- **Connectivity**: Internet connection is required during initial signup and cloud synchronization, but **zero internet is required for leaf diagnosis**.

---

## 2. First Launch Experience
When you open AgriCare AI for the first time:
1. **Splash Screen (Screen 01)**: You will see the animated green leaf scanner and AgriCare AI logo.
2. **Onboarding Carousel (Screen 02)**: You can swipe through three helpful introduction screens explaining how to scan leaves, apply remedies, and track recovery.
3. **Choose Language**: You can select English or বাংলা immediately or at any time in Settings.
4. **Login or Register**: You can create a new account or log in with your existing credentials.
