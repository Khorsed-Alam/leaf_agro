# AgriCare AI — Help and Feedback Guide

## 1. Help & FAQ Support Center (Screen 28)
If you have questions about using AgriCare AI, open Screen 28 from your Profile or Settings:
- **Visual Quick Guides**:
  1. *Taking Clear Leaf Photos*: Visual do's and don'ts for photography.
  2. *Understanding AI Confidence*: How to interpret percentages and colors.
  3. *Using Offline Mode*: Diagnosing crops without cellular internet.
  4. *Applying Treatment Safely*: Proper dosage mixing and spray safety gear.
- **Searchable FAQs**: Type keywords into the search bar to find answers to 12 common farming and app questions.
- **Contact Support**: Tap "Contact Agricultural Support" to reach certified extension contacts.

---

## 2. Submitting Feedback (Screen 29)
Your feedback helps agricultural researchers improve the application:
1. Tap **"Feedback"** from the Profile or Settings screen.
2. Select a star rating from 1 to 5 stars.
3. Select a category:
   - *AI prediction accuracy*
   - *App usability*
   - *Disease information*
   - *Treatment recommendations*
   - *Other*
4. Type your observations or suggestions in the text area (up to 2,000 characters).
5. Optionally attach a photo of the affected plant.
6. Tap **"Submit Feedback"**.
