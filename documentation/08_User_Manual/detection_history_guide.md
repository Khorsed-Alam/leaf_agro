# AgriCare AI — Detection History and Recovery Guide

## 1. Browsing Past Scans (Screen 22)
To review your previous scans:
1. Tap the **"History"** icon in the bottom navigation bar.
2. The dashboard displays all your past plant scans sorted chronologically.
3. You can filter records:
   - By **Crop** (e.g., show only Rice scans).
   - By **Date Range** (e.g., past 7 days, this month).
4. Tap any scan card to open the **History Details Screen (Screen 23)**.

---

## 2. Reviewing History Details (Screen 23)
On Screen 23, you can:
- Re-examine the original leaf photograph.
- Review the historical disease diagnosis and confidence score.
- Reopen the treatment and prevention guides.
- Delete the record if you no longer wish to keep it.
- Tap **"Monitor Recovery"** to open Screen 21.

---

## 3. Recording Plant Recovery Checkpoints (Screen 21)
After applying remedies, monitoring your plant's progress is simple:
1. Open Screen 21 from the result screen or the history details screen.
2. Review the chronological observation timeline.
3. Under **"Record Field Observation"**, select the current condition:
   - **Better (উন্নতি হয়েছে)**: Lesions have dried up, new shoots are green.
   - **No Change (একই রকম আছে)**: Spots remain unchanged.
   - **Worse (অবনতি হয়েছে)**: Lesions are expanding to other leaves.
   - **Not Sure (নিশ্চিত নই)**: Unclear progress.
4. Type your observation notes (e.g., "Sprayed Mancozeb on Monday. No new spots observed.").
5. Tap **"Save Checkpoint"**.
6. When you are ready for a definitive check, tap **"Scan Plant Again"** to take a fresh photo.
