# AgriCare AI — Account Registration and Login

## 1. Creating a Farmer Account (Screen 04)
To register a new profile:
1. On the Login screen (Screen 03), tap **"Create Account"** or **"নতুন একাউন্ট খুলুন"**.
2. Enter your **Full Name** (e.g., Md. Rafiqul Islam).
3. Enter your **Mobile Number** or **Email Address**.
4. Select your **Home District** from the dropdown list of 64 districts in Bangladesh (e.g., Bogura, Rajshahi, Dinajpur).
5. Choose a secure **Password** (at least 8 characters long).
6. Check the box to accept the Terms of Service & Privacy Policy.
7. Tap **"Register"**. Your account is created instantly, and you are directed to the Home Dashboard.

---

## 2. Logging In (Screen 03)
1. Enter your registered email or phone number.
2. Enter your password. You can tap the eye icon to reveal the password text.
3. Tap **"Login"** or **"লগইন করুন"**.

---

## 3. Password Recovery (Screen 05)
If you forget your password:
1. Tap **"Forgot Password?"** on the Login screen.
2. Enter your registered email address.
3. Check your email inbox for the reset link and follow the on-screen instructions to set a new password.
